Visual language · phase 1 · feeling & mood

A well-designed outdoor tool — that happens to know golf cold.

The system lives on top of the map. Chrome recedes so terrain, data and decisions come forward. Calm and roomy like Scandinavian design, exact and quiet like a precision instrument, and grounded in turf and weather rather than the clubhouse.

The feeling, tuned on three axes
Calmroomy, unhurried, restrained
Precisedata-confident, exact, engineered
